## combat-no-damage — fixed in `b012d57` after 19h

> I'm P1 and I'm trying to fight P2 and it's like P2's not reacting or taking any damage. And I think all of my attacks are hitting myself.

**Cause.** A revert on 2026-09-21 deleted resolveHitRegionAtHeight from FrameDataHitbox.ts and left the call to it inside checkCollision. Every connecting strike threw a ReferenceError, so no strike could deal damage on any build carrying it.

**Found by.** tsc --noEmit named it in one line; CI had been running that check and going red on main for four days.

**Lesson.** A gate that is green somewhere nobody reads is not a gate. Assert the symptom (damage reaches the opponent), not only that it compiles.

## combat-slow-motion — fixed in `61b8216` after 18h

> currently can only fire off 4 attack and it's the base ones ... P2's not reacting

**Cause.** The game loop clamped dt at 0.05s, so below 20fps every timer in the fight advanced slower than the wall clock. Attacks outlasted the gap between presses: of 16 presses, 10 arrived while the previous move was still running.

**Found by.** scripts/probe-damage-attribution.mjs — pressed the real keys, counted rising edges, and recorded the fighter's state at each one.

**Lesson.** Measure the CALL COUNT and the STATE at the input, not the code path.

## model-bind-space — fixed in `8e857f3` after 4.8d

> a lot of character models, GLBs and attires still having stretching and deformation on certain parts of their body

**Cause.** JAGER, JAGER_beard and TARZANIAN_DEVIL_skinned were bound ~0.85m from their own skeletons. Bind pose cancels the error exactly, so every existing check vouched for them.

**Found by.** tools/model_diag/bind_space.mjs — mean distance from a vertex to its dominant joint's bind position, offline over all 77 GLBs.

**Lesson.** A metric that cannot express the failure will vouch for it. skinqa scores a broken rig clean because it asks whether weights predict themselves.

## arm-to-hip-webbing — fixed in `23e01a4` after 1.6d

> on Wreck Patterson and Titan they still have parts of their arms attached to parts of their hips and legs ... fix that shit on all the models universally and put in whatever to keep it from ever happening again.

**Cause.** OPEN. The weight-level defect is real in the raw assets (WRECK_PATTERSON 180 bleeding verts, TITAN 136, worst pairs Hips~RightArm / Hips~LeftForeArm) and the load-time prune clears it completely (4/4 clean after the pipeline). The visible stretching is a separate thing: the share of the body bound to a DISTANT bone (WRECK 5.60%, godwithin 5.49%, TITAN 2.25%, VIPER — never reported — 0.33%).

**Tried and reverted.** A load-time repair rebinding far-bound vertices to the nearest bone SEGMENT. MEASURED WORSE and reverted: same probe, same clip, VIPER's stretch share went 3.21% -> 4.17% and WRECK's 2.14% -> 3.90%, because rebinding only the outliers leaves a seam against their untouched neighbours.

**Lesson.** THREE INDEPENDENT MEASUREMENTS NOW SAY THE WEIGHTS ARE CLEAN, and one of my own earlier numbers was wrong. (1) The live scene: scripts/probe-live-skin.mjs walks the RENDERED bodies in a real WRECK vs TITAN match and counts 0 bleeding vertices on both (12,035 and 11,469 verts), so the load-time prune reaches the screen, not just the test harness. (2) Bone-segment distance: WRECK's median is 16.9cm against VIPER's 17.1cm — indistinguishable from a model never complained about. (3) My earlier 'WRECK 5.60% vs VIPER 0.33% far-bound' was an ARTIFACT of measuring distance to the joint ORIGIN; a joint sits at one END of its bone, so an ankle vertex is legitimately 0.4m from the knee that drives it, and the 'defect' was almost entirely legs. That also explains why the rebind repair made stretching worse: it was moving correctly-weighted vertices. Posed stretching does remain, on EVERY model including ones never reported (VIPER's worst edge growth is larger than WRECK's), so whatever is visible in the screenshots is not cross-body skin weights and needs the owner to point at the exact body part, or a look at the rendering side rather than the rig.

## open-source-animation-dead — fixed in `70e346f` after 19h

> pull in any open source so you can actually see and test and play ... pull stuff from open source that'll get our fights actually going well

**Cause.** The Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0-1.0, commercially safe) was fetched, unpacked and listed in OpenAnimationSources.generated.ts, and contributed ZERO clips. Two bugs: the bake filtered files with /\\.glb$/ — a regex requiring a literal BACKSLASH before the extension, which no POSIX path has, so nothing matched and it reported zero skips and zero errors; and once found, every clip failed the SkeletonUtils cross-check because three's retargetClip reads target.skeleton.bones unconditionally and the canonical target is a bare bone tree.

**Found by.** The bake report said quaterniusSources: 0 while three GLBs sat in vendor/. A silent 100% loss with no skip and no error recorded.

**Lesson.** A pipeline that reports zero skips AND zero output is not idle, it is broken. Count what went IN, not just what came out.

## attacks-do-not-travel — fixed in `5bc31ef` after 20h

> combat is a bit smoother, but it's still not on the level of Tekken ... I can tell you're still hand tooling things when I told you to pull in open source for these fixes ... actually pull stuff from [the Schwarzerblitz repo]

**Cause.** Attack displacement came from ATTACK_ROOT_MOTION_PROFILES, a hand-written table of FIVE entries keyed by animation name, while the imported Schwarzerblitz move graph carries authored per-frame root motion on 127 of its 133 moves that nothing read. The importer also discarded the '>' interpolation flag (165 of 169 movement lines carry it), so every authored travel would have been read as a single-frame teleport. Root motion was also armed for P1 only — the opponent never travelled with an attack at all.

**Found by.** Counted engine consumption of every field in the imported graph: hidden 0, animationRightSide 0, range 0, newStance 0, movement 0. Semantics then read out of the Schwarzerblitz C++ (FK_Move, FK_MoveFileParser, FK_Character) rather than guessed.

**Lesson.** RESOLVED UNIVERSALLY. Two independent sources of travel existed and neither reached the game: Schwarzerblitz authors it in #MOVEMENT (its animations are rotation-only by design), and our own captures perform it in a `pose` block of joint world positions that nothing read — 273 of 973 source clips travel more than 15cm, up to 1.22m. Both now feed ONE cumulative travel curve (src/engine/motion/RootTravel.ts) sampled between two instants, so it is frame-rate independent by construction. Read at BAKE time from public/motion, so every future clip gets it by existing rather than by somebody remembering to wire it up. Orientation comes from the shoulder axis and is pinned by a test against moves whose names state their direction (BackStep negative, DROP_KICK positive), because an inverted sign keeps every magnitude believable and only shows up as fighters retreating when they attack.

## back-attacks-never-hit — fixed in `454c782` after 21h

> when you press back and attack, it's like kind of doing something where it's making you keep walking back. So when you do back and attack, none of those attacks ever hit. In Tekken and Schwarzerblitz it's more specific than that.

**Cause.** NOT the attack carrying you — measured strictly between the start and end of the attack, movement is 0.000m on every input including back, so the state machine anchors you correctly. The fighter walked OUT OF RANGE before the attack's active frames arrived. Scale-free comparison: Schwarzerblitz crosses 1.23 of its own strike reaches per second of walking (80 units/s over a 65-unit modal range); we crossed 1.57 (2.2 m/s over a MEASURED 1.40m reach).

**Found by.** scripts/audit-combat-feel.mjs — real presses in a real match, measuring displacement, connection and the move that fired per input.

**Lesson.** TWO OF MY OWN READINGS WERE WRONG BEFORE THE RIGHT ONE. First I timed drift over a fixed window around the press and reported 1.76m of 'the attack carrying you' — most of it was legitimate walking, because holding back is supposed to walk you back. Then I took the strike reach as 0.8m from the distance another test happened to swing at, which made the pace gap look like 2.2x and produced a 1.0 m/s walk that was sluggish rather than deliberate. Driving every attack out to its furthest connecting distance says all four reach 1.40m and the real gap was 1.28x. MEASURE THE THING, INCLUDING THE DENOMINATOR.

## stance-system-unread — fixed in `3786b95` after 1.5d

> we need to pull more stuff from Tekken and Schwarzerblitz ... so it feels like something built on top of Tekken and classic fighting games

**Cause.** 50 imported moves declare the stance they END in (#NEWSTANCE) and nothing read it. Stance was derived from the pad every frame — Crouch while you hold down, Ground the instant you release — so a move could never LEAVE you anywhere. That made the genre's whole grounded/rising/wake-up layer unreachable: Ukemi (breakfall to standing), SupineReversal (up off your back), sweeps that end crouching, dashes that end running.

**Found by.** tools/audit/unconsumed_fields.mjs — the consumption gate built after the same shape of bug appeared three times. SbMove.newStance was in its baseline as a named gap, and dropped off the report on its own once the field had a reader.

**Lesson.** A move-imposed stance must survive a NEUTRAL pad but yield to a deliberate input, or it is a trap rather than a position. And the first version of the test called the state machine without attaching a command buffer, so the special could never match, a bare press fell through to the DEFAULT light attack (which carries no end stance), and a correct implementation looked broken. The test now asserts the move under test actually ran.

## combat-does-not-flow — fixed in `23e01a4` after 13h

> the combat needs to be more ... so it feels like something built on top of Tekken and classic fighting games ... we really need to research and pull from code and stuff in those games to get a better flow going

**Cause.** NO MOVE A PLAYER FIRES COULD BE CANCELLED. detectCancel reads cancelInto/followups off the current move; the 26 generated directional slots carried neither, and neither do the four DEFAULT_MOVE_WINDOWS. So every attack ran to completion: throw one, sit through its whole recovery, throw the next. That is the difference between a fighting game and a sequence of single hits.

**Found by.** Checked whether the moves the player actually fires carry the cancel data the source authors — the same 'imported but never read' shape that has now appeared four times this session.

**Lesson.** The WINDOW SHAPE is measured (213 authored windows across 133 imported moves open at a median 0.20 of the move) but the PAIRING is a derived default, because the source authors named strings per move and generated slots have none. Same direction, other button — punch into kick and back. One link, not a licence to cancel into anything, so it cannot become a mash.

## blend-keyed-by-destination — fixed in `05b3049` after 14h

> ours are keyed by destination rather than by from->to pair — worth doing. And get all fixed and bugs with model stretching tearing and ugly unblended animations ... fixed so we have a better game base

**Cause.** FADE_DURATIONS was keyed only by the destination state, so a snap out of a hit reaction and a long settle out of a run got the same duration entering the same idle. Measured over 714 real clip transitions, the pose distance between them spans 20 to 68 degrees — a spread one key cannot express.

**Found by.** Read the Namco Cancel struct in Kiloutre/TKMovesets (cloned, not paraphrased) and measured our own corpus's transition distances.

**Lesson.** COMPUTED BEATS TABULATED when the table would be 200,000 invented cells. The distance is already knowable from the live skeleton against the incoming clip's first frame. TWO THINGS CHECKED AND FOUND NOT TO APPLY: the Lerp-vs-Slerp diagnosis (1,570 hemisphere flips exist, but every interpolation here goes through THREE.Quaternion.slerp which corrects the sign, and there are no raw quaternion lerps) and LBS candy-wrapping (not yet measured — the remaining posed stretching is present on every model including ones never reported). STILL OPEN from the reference: Tekken's Cancel carries `starting_frame`, the frame the TARGET move begins at when cancelled into, which lets a string's second hit flow instead of replaying its startup. We have no data to fill it.
