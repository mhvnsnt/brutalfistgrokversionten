## combat-no-damage — fixed in `b012d57` after 19h

> I'm P1 and I'm trying to fight P2 and it's like P2's not reacting or taking any damage. And I think all of my attacks are hitting myself.

**Cause.** A revert on 2026-09-21 deleted resolveHitRegionAtHeight from FrameDataHitbox.ts and left the call to it inside checkCollision. Every connecting strike threw a ReferenceError, so no strike could deal damage on any build carrying it.

**Found by.** tsc --noEmit named it in one line; CI had been running that check and going red on main for four days.

**Lesson.** A gate that is green somewhere nobody reads is not a gate. Assert the symptom (damage reaches the opponent), not only that it compiles.

## combat-slow-motion — fixed in `61b8216` after 18h

> currently can only fire off 4 attack and it's the base ones ... P2's not reacting

**Cause.** The game loop clamped dt at 0.05s, so below 20fps every timer in the fight advanced slower than the wall clock. Attacks outlasted the gap between presses: of 16 presses, 10 arrived while the previous move was still running.

**Found by.** scripts/probe-damage-attribution.mjs — pressed the real keys, counted rising edges, and recorded the fighter's state at each one.

**Lesson.** Measure the CALL COUNT and the STATE at the input, not the code path.

## model-bind-space — fixed in `8e857f3` after 4.8d

> a lot of character models, GLBs and attires still having stretching and deformation on certain parts of their body

**Cause.** JAGER, JAGER_beard and TARZANIAN_DEVIL_skinned were bound ~0.85m from their own skeletons. Bind pose cancels the error exactly, so every existing check vouched for them.

**Found by.** tools/model_diag/bind_space.mjs — mean distance from a vertex to its dominant joint's bind position, offline over all 77 GLBs.

**Lesson.** A metric that cannot express the failure will vouch for it. skinqa scores a broken rig clean because it asks whether weights predict themselves.

## arm-to-hip-webbing — OPEN, 20h old

> on Wreck Patterson and Titan they still have parts of their arms attached to parts of their hips and legs ... fix that shit on all the models universally and put in whatever to keep it from ever happening again.

**Cause.** OPEN. The weight-level defect is real in the raw assets (WRECK_PATTERSON 180 bleeding verts, TITAN 136, worst pairs Hips~RightArm / Hips~LeftForeArm) and the load-time prune clears it completely (4/4 clean after the pipeline). The visible stretching is a separate thing: the share of the body bound to a DISTANT bone (WRECK 5.60%, godwithin 5.49%, TITAN 2.25%, VIPER — never reported — 0.33%).

**Tried and reverted.** A load-time repair rebinding far-bound vertices to the nearest bone SEGMENT. MEASURED WORSE and reverted: same probe, same clip, VIPER's stretch share went 3.21% -> 4.17% and WRECK's 2.14% -> 3.90%, because rebinding only the outliers leaves a seam against their untouched neighbours.

**Lesson.** Rebinding a subset of a mesh's vertices is not a repair — the discontinuity costs more than the lever it removes. The next attempt should transfer a whole proven rig rather than patch vertices.

## open-source-animation-dead — fixed in `70e346f` after 19h

> pull in any open source so you can actually see and test and play ... pull stuff from open source that'll get our fights actually going well

**Cause.** The Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0-1.0, commercially safe) was fetched, unpacked and listed in OpenAnimationSources.generated.ts, and contributed ZERO clips. Two bugs: the bake filtered files with /\\.glb$/ — a regex requiring a literal BACKSLASH before the extension, which no POSIX path has, so nothing matched and it reported zero skips and zero errors; and once found, every clip failed the SkeletonUtils cross-check because three's retargetClip reads target.skeleton.bones unconditionally and the canonical target is a bare bone tree.

**Found by.** The bake report said quaterniusSources: 0 while three GLBs sat in vendor/. A silent 100% loss with no skip and no error recorded.

**Lesson.** A pipeline that reports zero skips AND zero output is not idle, it is broken. Count what went IN, not just what came out.

## attacks-do-not-travel — fixed in `83f179c` after 20h

> combat is a bit smoother, but it's still not on the level of Tekken ... I can tell you're still hand tooling things when I told you to pull in open source for these fixes ... actually pull stuff from [the Schwarzerblitz repo]

**Cause.** Attack displacement came from ATTACK_ROOT_MOTION_PROFILES, a hand-written table of FIVE entries keyed by animation name, while the imported Schwarzerblitz move graph carries authored per-frame root motion on 127 of its 133 moves that nothing read. The importer also discarded the '>' interpolation flag (165 of 169 movement lines carry it), so every authored travel would have been read as a single-frame teleport. Root motion was also armed for P1 only — the opponent never travelled with an attack at all.

**Found by.** Counted engine consumption of every field in the imported graph: hidden 0, animationRightSide 0, range 0, newStance 0, movement 0. Semantics then read out of the Schwarzerblitz C++ (FK_Move, FK_MoveFileParser, FK_Character) rather than guessed.

**Lesson.** STILL OPEN DOWNSTREAM, and now the blocker is named. NO CLIP IN THE PROJECT CARRIES ROOT TRANSLATION: both generated motion banks store rotations only (rx/ry/rz per bone, no positions), so clip-derived travel is not available without re-importing from the original FBX/GLB with positions preserved. That is by design on the Schwarzerblitz side — its animations are rotation-only and the root motion lives in the move file's #MOVEMENT block, which is exactly what this change imports. So the authored travel now in the engine is the ONLY travel that exists, and it does not fire, because our own generated movesets cover all 26 directional slots and win every match. Two ways forward: let a Schwarzerblitz move win the inputs it defines (cheap, but costs some of the per-fighter variety the generated sets were built for), or preserve hips translation at import so every clip carries its own footwork (the real fix, and the one that would also give the 455 baked clips real travel).
