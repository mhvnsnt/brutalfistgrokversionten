# The King of Fighters XV — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: SNK (producer Yasuyuki Oda; creative director Eisuke Ogura; game director Kaito Soranaka)
- Genre / platforms: 3v3 team fighting game / PS4, PS5, PC (Steam/Epic), Xbox One/Series
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): SNK's stated priority is longevity through community support, not launch-and-drop. Oda developed XV "to be played for many years to come"; director Kaito Soranaka says SNK won't cut games off early because "there are a lot longer tails" with digital sales — it is "very shortsighted to cut things off too early." Rollback netcode was added (and retro-fitted to Samurai Shodown, KOF '98 UM, 2002 UM) precisely because "games that have rollback are played for a long time." On new audiences: "I'm always thinking of ways we can open the barrier to entry even by a little bit," while simultaneously "cater[ing] to fans who have been with us for years." The roster philosophy: beloved characters treated well — "the reason why these franchises are so loved even today is because they treated the characters well."
- Key quotes:
  - "There’s absolutely a need to acquire newer and newer audiences. If we don’t pull that off, then the game will usher in its last update only after a few years. That being said, if we don’t cater to fans who have been with us for years, then there’s also no point in continuing the series." — Kaito Soranaka, Game Director, Player2 interview (via DashFight, ~2022)
  - "We really wanted to implement rollback earlier... Another thing we took into account is that games that have rollback are played for a long time and we want people to keep playing Samsho, we want it to be a game that lasts for a longer period of time." — Yasuyuki Oda, Producer, SNK community Q&A (via Neo Geo Forever forum)
  - "Doing that as much as possible and making sure the community keeps growing, that is how we can continue to create new fans for the products and IP." — Kaito Soranaka, Game Director, on adding rollback to retro titles, Dot Esports (~2022)
  - "The fighters on the NEOGEO all had really popular characters. I think the reason why these franchises are so loved even today is because they treated the characters well." — Yasuyuki Oda, Producer, Retro Gamer interview (via GamesRadar, 2021) [unverified]
- Why players love/support it: The 3v3 team format's distinct strategic identity, a huge roster of returning fan-favorites ("fresh and festive feel" per SNK), improved-from-XIV visuals, day-one rollback netcode that let global communities (Latin America especially) actually play together, and SNK's long-tail patch/DLC support plus grassroots esports programs.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Classic premium + DLC fighters/costumes model; SNK's philosophy is retention-first ("supporting its players long-term is everything," per Dot Esports interview framing): keep the game playable and competitive for years so the IP keeps gaining fans, rather than squeezing a short sales window.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Treat characters like people fans will love for decades, not content to burn through — and invest in the invisible infrastructure (netcode/online) because "games that have [it] are played for a long time." Long-tail support beats launch-week extraction.
