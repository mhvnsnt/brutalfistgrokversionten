# Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio (SEGA); chief producer Hiroyuki Sakamoto; studio director Masayoshi Yokoyama
- Genre / platforms: Turn-based action-command RPG + dense open-city life sim; PS5/PS4/Xbox Series/Xbox One/PC (2024)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): A realistic life-experience story wrapped in absurd side content — "The devs at RGG Studio [and] story writing team are a bunch of comedy lovers... the contrast between the serious and very funny aspects of it. I see that as a signature of this game, in this franchise." Characters age in real time ("Every release, Kiryu gets older... They're not like Superman"), and every location choice serves the story first: "Everything hinges on the story. If the game taking place outside of Japan will enhance that story and make it fun, then we'll definitely go that route." The balance target is explicit: "We really try to focus on the balance between good gameplay and a good story."
- Key quotes: (2-4; each: "quote" — Name, Role, Publication/Event, Year; append [unverified] if from a search snippet not a fetched page)
  - "One of the things that make RGG Studio and Like a Dragon special is the contrast between the serious and very funny aspects of it. I see that as a signature of this game, in this franchise." — Hiroyuki Sakamoto, Chief Producer, TheGamer, 2024
  - "We're following this character getting older and hitting those different life stages. That's the kind of story that we want to tell. Everybody goes through different stages in life... We want to illustrate that and tell that very realistic life experience story." — Hiroyuki Sakamoto, Chief Producer, TheGamer, 2024
  - "We really try to focus on what we want to make and try to stay true to what we want to make because we don't want outside feedback or opinions to influence our decision-making." — Hiroyuki Sakamoto, Chief Producer, TheGamer, 2024
  - "Side stories in Like a Dragon titles have always been optional. You can progress through only the main story if you want to, and you can play the side stories to feel the volume too... if you attempt to clear everything, it may never end." — Masayoshi Yokoyama, Studio Director, Automaton-Media (via GamingBolt), 2024 [unverified]
- Why players love/support it: Kiryu's bucket-list arc gave longtime fans an emotional farewell they feared would be bleak — "We don't want Kiryu to rot away... The story has a lot of hope." Dondoko Island and the Sujimon/crazy-delivery side games are dense enough to be "independent games" on their own, and the dual Ichiban/Kiryu leads give "different perspectives in the game, enriching the story."
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Full-price premium; no live-service hooks. The dev-stated model is volume: make the side content so substantial that completion feels "never-ending" — value justifies the purchase. "We have to have the success of the games to have the next game. So that doesn't change."
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Contrast serious drama with genuinely stupid-fun side content — that's "a signature." Let characters age and hit life stages in real time, and build side content dense enough that it feels like "it may never end," but always optional.
