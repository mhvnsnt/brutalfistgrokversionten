# Dragon Quest XI — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Square Enix (series creator/designer/writer Yuji Horii; producer Hokuto Okamoto; director Takeshi Uchikawa; PS4 version co-developed with Orca; music Koichi Sugiyama; art Akira Toriyama)
- Genre / platforms: Traditional turn-based JRPG; PS4, 3DS (Japan), PC, Switch (XI S Definitive Edition), Xbox
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Total accessibility plus depth — the "rail" of a clear story means "players had this problem of not quite knowing what to do," so Horii built a followable scenario and turn-based battles: "this approach is why the Dragon Quest games are easy to play for everyone, and that's the strength that we have." Turn-based combat is deliberately kept so "everyone" can "experience it to the very end." Add direct fan responsiveness: "the players wanted to choose their partners more freely... that was based directly on fan feedback," plus orchestrated music and DQVIII costumes in XI S from overseas demand.
- Key quotes: (2-4; each: "quote" — Name, Role, Publication/Event, Year; append [unverified] if from a search snippet not a fetched page)
  - "I created a scenario, a story, that the players can follow that acts as like a rail for them to follow. I also made the battles turn-based... I think that approach is why the Dragon Quest games are easy to play for everyone, and that's the strength that we have." — Yuji Horii, Series Creator/Designer, RPG Site interview, 2019
  - "We did refer to some reviews and fan comments that we had, yes! One of them was that the players wanted to choose their partners more freely... that was based directly on fan feedback and comments." — Yuji Horii, Series Creator/Designer, RPG Site interview, 2019
  - "When you're walking around the world and if you got lost we want to ensure that getting lost wasn't a waste. You might find something at a dead end of a dungeon." — Yuji Horii, Series Creator/Designer, Siliconera interview, 2018 [unverified]
  - "I like playing jokes and pranks on people — so even when the story is super serious, I like to get the funny stuff in there." — Yuji Horii, Series Creator/Designer, RPG Site interview, 2019
- Why players love/support it: It can be "enjoyed 100% without any knowledge of past titles" while rewarding veterans with homage (Toriyama boss art, past music); "not create any useless characters" was the team's internal message, so players bond with the whole party; and Horii keeps inventing within the formula (fun-sized forge, Draconian Quest) while spin-offs absorb the wild experiments — the core never breaks.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Explicitly anti-DLC for XI — Dengeki PlayStation roundup confirmed "no plans for DLC" (Siliconera/NintendoEverything, 2017). Value comes from one complete package; the Definitive Edition (XI S) is the premium upsell: new stories, 2D mode, orchestrated music — priced as a second full release.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Make the core playable by everyone (clear rails + forgiving systems), then hide real rewards for wanderers — "getting lost wasn't a waste." Ship one complete game; sell the definitive edition later with genuinely new content.
