# Elden Ring — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: FromSoftware (publisher: Bandai Namco)
- Genre / platforms: Open-world dark fantasy action RPG; PS4/PS5, Xbox One/Series X|S, PC (2022)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Director Hidetaka Miyazaki frames Elden Ring as "a challenging game, not a difficult game": players return because challenge is designed to be overcome through cunning, study, and learning from mistakes — and because the open world's breadth of freedom means an impasse is never a wall; you can come back to something later and approach it at your own pace. Exploration and discovery were the top priority "above everything else," with threats, dangers, and unknowns deliberately preserved so discoveries feel genuinely discovered. The connective tissue is trust: the team trusts players to overcome challenges and make discoveries, and that trust creates a healthy landscape for adventure. Players also return because the studio treats its audience as collaborators in uncovering secrets — Miyazaki has said he and the devs are "always surprised and delighted by how much the players do discover, and how much these communities work to uncover these secrets," even teasing one element he felt had not yet been discovered.
- Key quotes:
  - "I'd like new players to feel unpressured and that they can approach the game at their pace... They can come back to something later when they're at an impasse so that they can have this freedom of progression and not have to bang their head against a wall over and over." — Hidetaka Miyazaki, Director, PlayStation Blog (via PC Gamer), 2022
  - "We don't set out to create a difficult game. We set out to create a challenging game. And in order to achieve that, we need there to be threats and dangers, and we need there to be unknowns." — Hidetaka Miyazaki, Director, IGN interview (2024), via mirror
  - "For Elden Ring, there needed to be another thing: adventure. A feeling of exploration... was the top priority above everything else." — Hidetaka Miyazaki, Director, IGN interview (2024), via mirror
  - "Our main idea is just to trust players. We trust that they'll overcome these challenges and we trust that they'll make these discoveries. And I think giving them trust just creates a healthy landscape for them to play and adventure." — Hidetaka Miyazaki, Director, IGN interview (2024), via mirror
- Why players love/support it: A massive world built around player discovery rather than checklists; fair, earned victories that make the cycle of retrying feel enjoyable in itself; builds and playstyles that keep encounters fresh; an always-evolving lore that rewards community investigation; a studio with a consistent, reliable creative voice that never talks down to its audience.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No on-record statement found about monetization philosophy. ANALYSIS: Elden Ring shipped as a complete premium game with no microtransactions; a substantial paid DLC (Shadow of the Erdtree) followed in 2024. The studio's posture is value-through-completeness: players pay once for the whole game, and return for the depth, not for purchased extras.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Build challenge that players can walk away from and come back to — freedom of progression beats a hard wall every time; protect the sense of discovery (real unknowns, not map-icon errands) and trust players to figure things out instead of hand-holding them.
