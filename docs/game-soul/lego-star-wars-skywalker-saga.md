# LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: TT Games (Strategic Director / Head of Production: Jonathan Smith); with Lucasfilm Games (Managing Producer: Craig Derrick); Warner Bros. Games
- Genre / platforms: Action-adventure / open-hub; PS4/PS5, Xbox One/Series, Switch, PC (2022)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Freedom of choice is the stated core principle — players can start from ANY of the nine films and play the saga in whatever order they want: "stories exist to be played with, however the player wants" (Jonathan Smith, StarWars.com). The retention loop is volume × charm: nearly 500 characters, a full galaxy of open hubs, Kyber Brick progression across character classes, and free-play mode to remix adventures with any unlocked character. Craig Derrick: the game had to be "massive because it had to be" — scope is the value proposition, with the trademark LEGO "charm and humor" as the emotional constant.
- Key quotes:
  - "Freedom of choice is a really important principle for us! We love stories and storytelling; but we also feel, both as gamers ourselves and with particular concern for young players driven to experimentation and change, that stories exist to be played with, however the player wants." — Jonathan Smith, Strategic Director/Head of Production, TT Games, StarWars.com interview (reported via Female First/CGM), 2020
  - "We needed the game to feel epic in scope and scale while bringing the player intimately closer to the action like never before. This required new technology, new game mechanics, and a passionate team of developers to create a cast of hundreds and all the familiar worlds they populate — all with the charm and humor you expect with a LEGO Star Wars game. Quite frankly, this game is massive because it had to be." — Craig Derrick, Managing Producer, Lucasfilm Games, StarWars.com interview (reported via GameWatcher), 2020
  - "There are nearly 500 characters in this game, with many of them playable. When choosing characters for a game this large, we just looked at every film in the saga and tried to say 'yes' as much as possible." — Craig Derrick, Managing Producer, Lucasfilm Games, StarWars.com interview (reported via GameWatcher), 2020
  - "Whether they are existing fans of the franchise or discovering it for the first time, everyone will enjoy the epic story of all nine films with all the humor and charm that LEGO games bring." — Jonathan Smith, Strategic Director/Head of Production, TT Games, launch statement, 2022
- Why players love/support it: The definitive all-nine-films package — fans can play their favorite trilogy first; humor undercuts any self-seriousness (players keep playing for the jokes); drop-in/drop-out co-op makes it the default family/friends game; 300+ unlockable characters and Kyber Brick progression systems reward completionists; charm is the glue holding the massive scope together.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No on-record monetization philosophy from TT Games found. The model: premium full-price release, with paid character DLC packs (Mandalorian, Rogue One, Classic Characters) as the post-launch revenue tier — scope first, DLC adds fan-service characters.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): "Say yes as much as possible" — breadth of roster and content is the value proposition for a franchise game; pair it with freedom of choice (any character, any order, any entry point) and humor, which turns a giant scope from overwhelming into inviting.
