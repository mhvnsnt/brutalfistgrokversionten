# EA Sports FC Mobile — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: EA Sports (launched Sept 2023, successor to FIFA Mobile; major seasonal updates FC Mobile 26, 27)
- Genre / platforms: Mobile football simulation / iOS, Android
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Approachable-yet-authentic mobile football — refined mobile controls for all skill levels, "True Player Personality" giving real players authentic in-game character, and a live, ever-evolving service (annual Season Updates, Gauntlet Mode, Game Plans, PlayStyles, fan-voted anniversary content) that mirrors real-world football. EA's stated philosophy is engagement first, revenue follows.
- Key quotes:
  - "By honing in on what makes mobile gameplay special with our refined controls, we've created a football experience that's approachable to all players and skill levels. With True Player Personality bringing authentic player characteristics in-game, we encourage our own community of players to discover their own style of play and celebrate the joy of glory alongside their favorite football stars." — Lawrence Koh, VP, EA SPORTS FC MOBILE, FC Mobile launch press release, 2023
  - "From the overhauled gameplay... that's driven by player feedback, to fan-voted content in FC Mobile, we're co-creating a global football community that is more expressive, dynamic, and committed than ever before." — EA SPORTS FC 26 launch statement, 2025
  - "Our mobile strategy is focused on leaning into massive online communities, investing behind concentrated bets on standalone titles with breakout hit potential all while driving profitable growth... the company sees potential in mobile to increase monetisation through extended player engagement." — Stuart Canfield, CFO, EA Investor Day, 2024 [per PocketGamer.biz coverage; FC Mobile cited as the example]
- Why players love/support it: Licensed authenticity (real clubs, players, Champions League), building a dream Ultimate Team, tactical control, free access to console-grade football in pocket form; live MLS simulcast inside the app (FCM TV) blurs game and sport.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Engagement-first: "[we are] deepening that relationship with our players, and making sure that they are playing more often and for longer. In terms of the revenue, this is consistent with EA strategy overall – if we engage our consumers, then the finances tend to take care of itself." — Todd Sitrin, EA SVP/GM Competitive Gaming, MCV, 2017 (EA competitive-gaming strategy, applies to the FC ecosystem)
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Authenticity + co-creation: let players discover their own play style and vote on content (fan-voted rewards), and tie the game world to real events so it never stagnates. Make it approachable first; engagement monetizes itself.
