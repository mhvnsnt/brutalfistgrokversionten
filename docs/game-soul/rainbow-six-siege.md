# Rainbow Six Siege — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Ubisoft Montreal
- Genre / platforms: Tactical team FPS (live service); PC, PlayStation, Xbox (2015; Siege X refresh 2025; free-to-play)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Sheer, unmasterable depth — a game you can't master in 10 years, so there's always another discovery on the journey; a creative tactical sandbox (destruction, asymmetric operators) where every round is unique and knowledge is as valuable as aim; the team itself doubled down on the game after a soft launch, and that dedication — plus being staffed by real players at every rank who listen to the community — shone through; a "serve the players who stay like kings and queens" golden rule for loyalty.
- Key quotes:
  - "I think one of the big points is just the sheer depth of the game. Like even within 10 years, I don't think you'll be able to master a game like Siege and as a gamer that's incredibly rewarding. It means that you're on a journey to learn, master and discover new things with a game that's always changing." — Alexander Karpazis, Creative Director, Rainbow Six Siege X, IGN (via Next Level News, 2025)
  - "It really comes down to listening to the community. They'll always be vocal in what they want to see and how things change... it's not a matter of mastering where we are right now. It's being comfortable and knowing that we still have to listen to our community, we still have to adapt, and we still have to innovate as well." — Alexander Karpazis, Creative Director, Rainbow Six Siege X, IGN (via Next Level News, 2025)
  - "Our golden rule is to serve the players who stay like kings and queens." [unverified] — Joshua Mills, Creative Director, Rainbow Six Siege, The Game Business (via VGC, 2026; search snippet only)
  - "When it first came out, it wasn't the huge smashing success that it is now. It needed some work and the team doubled down on it, and that kind of dedication, I think, is ultimately the thing that shone through and really put us where we are today." [unverified] — Alexander Karpazis, Creative Director, Rainbow Six Siege X, GameRant 10-year interview (2026; search snippet only)
- Why players love/support it: The only mainstream shooter where outsmarting someone beats out-aiming them; the adrenaline of a 1vX clutch in a destructible, drone-scouted building is unmatched; a deep operator roster and an esports scene give casuals and pros a shared language; Siege X's free-to-play pivot brought a record wave of new players, proving the formula still converts.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): On Siege X going free-to-play, Karpazis said: "For us, we're seeing the benefits already. We have more new players joining the game than ever before since launch, and that's exactly what we'd hoped for." — IGN (via Next Level News, 2025). Cosmetics/season passes are the model; no pay-to-win claims from the devs on record.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Build a game nobody can fully master — depth IS retention. And adopt the golden rule: double down on the players who stay instead of chasing the ones who left; reward loyalty publicly and loudly (new content drops during competitor launches, veteran rewards).
