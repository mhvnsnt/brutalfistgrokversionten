# Baldur's Gate 3 — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Larian Studios
- Genre / platforms: Party-based cinematic CRPG (Dungeons & Dragons); PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, macOS (2023)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Larian's design philosophy is, in CEO Swen Vincke's words, "player first, always": treat the player like you would want to be treated, never build on rails ("If it's on rails, you can go to a theme park; this is not a theme park"). Players keep returning because the game is a systems-driven sandbox where their own creativity — talk, sneak, teleport, shove, stack explosives — is the gameplay, and consequences genuinely diverge (lead writer Adam Smith notes the evil/Dark Urge second playthrough changes the whole shape of the run). Writing director Alena Dubrovina says they wanted "exciting choices as a player, visually and narratively," even for players who know nothing about D&D. The studio also iterated in public through Early Access, visibly incorporating player feedback — the game keeps improving with the community rather than apart from it.
- Key quotes:
  - "I started my summit here by literally telling [Larian], treat the player like you would want to be treated... If it's on rails, you can go to a theme park; this is not a theme park." — Swen Vincke, CEO, PC Gamer first-anniversary interview, 2024 [unverified]
  - "We only do this if we don't compromise for it. If any of that is necessary, then we don't do it." — Swen Vincke, CEO, on going cinematic without cutting player choice, quoted by writing director Adam Smith in Edge/GamesRadar making-of feature, 2024
  - "We really wanted to make it so that even if you don't know D&D or Baldur's Gate, you would still have exciting choices as a player, visually and narratively, and be able to enjoy the experience." — Alena Dubrovina, Art Director, Edge/GamesRadar making-of feature, 2024
  - "The ethos of Larian is very much 'keep doing it until it's good'... You could say, 'OK, it's good enough'." — Adam Smith, Writing Director, Edge/GamesRadar making-of feature, 2024 (on Larian refusing to settle for "good enough")
- Why players love/support it: Total player agency — choices, builds, and romances differ radically between runs; companions with real emotional weight; humor and reactivity that make the world feel alive; no microtransactions; and a developer that communicates openly and treats players as collaborators.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No on-record statement found about monetization per se. ANALYSIS: BG3 shipped complete with zero microtransactions and free substantial post-launch patches; Vincke has publicly positioned creative independence ("Take risks and stay independent") and full-game value over recurring revenue mechanics.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Make choice real — let players solve missions multiple ways and honor consequences on replay; treat the player like you'd want to be treated, which means shipping a complete game with zero pay-to-play walls.
