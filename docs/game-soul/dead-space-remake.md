# Dead Space (2023 remake) — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Motive Studio (EA); senior producer Philippe Ducharme; creative director Roman Campos-Oriola
- Genre / platforms: Survival horror; PS5, Xbox Series X/S, PC (2023)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Unbroken immersion + an AI that fights familiarity. Ducharme: "Now you're not traversing through trams or having breaks between chapters, that was something that we wanted to do to increase the immersion. Dead Space was an amazing game for immersion, how can we push that?" The unbroken Ishimura needed the "Intensity Director" — "hundreds of possible scenarios" of lighting, audio, and encounters on re-traversal so the game "stays scary, even for areas that have already been explored." The studio's remake ethic: "If it ain't broke, don't fix it... leaving alone the beats that made the game so great, and improving the ones that were problematic" (lead level designer Catherine Stewart). Ducharme's caution: "If we want the tension, then we keep those timings. It's not just that we should do it because we can do it" — changes had to map to pillars, "not just make changes because we thought we knew better than the original team, because they did an amazing job." And the immersion metric: "If we're able to get the player with the pad in their hands at the start and play throughout the entire game without putting their pad down... that's because they jumped in and they don't want to leave... then job done."

- Why players love/support it: The dismemberment combat sandbox, seamless ship exploration, and the Intensity Director means no two playthroughs are identical — New Game+ actively replays differently. Side quests added depth to side characters (Temple, Cross) the original only mentioned in audio logs.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Premium single-purchase; no on-record dev monetization philosophy found.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): An AI "intensity director" that remixes encounters on backtracking is the cheapest replayability system — scripted first, reactive later. And the change discipline: never change a beat the original got right just because you can.
