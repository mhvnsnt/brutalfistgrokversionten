# Last War: Survival — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: First Fun (Beijing; founder Xie Xian Lin, ex-CTO of Elex Technology; Tencent held ~10% stake at founding) — also known as Rivergame / FUNFLY PTE. LTD.
- Genre / platforms: 4X zombie-survival strategy — iOS, Android
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): ANALYSIS (no on-record dev statements found). By design evidence: the genius is a two-game onion — a viral hypercasual "second game" (math gates, bomb-defusal saga) pulled straight from the UA creatives sits ON TOP of a classic 4X, and the onboarding delays the 4X reveal until the player is invested. The 4X layer itself is then streamlined via a "world quest" scanner so non-4X players can approach it; endgame retention is pure guild warfare and live events. Revenue crossed ~$2.8M/day and $2B+ lifetime on this formula.

- Why players love/support it: Instant-fun onboarding that feels like the ads (rare honesty in mobile UA), streamlined 4X map via the radar/scanner, clanmates helping world quests, Plunder mechanics keeping early heroes relevant late — the whole feature set feels cohesive from ad to endgame. Massive event/live ops cadence.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No on-record statements found. ANALYSIS: 4X-native — heavy event-driven offers, hero collection, alliance-war power scaling; enormous UA spend (Soul Metro Station offline ads etc.) funded by deep whale monetization; the design keeps ARPPU high enough to outbid everyone else on the same creatives.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Make the ad IS the game — build your marketing's viral hook as a real playable mode that persists into the late game (not a funnel lie), and streamline the complex core so non-genre players can reach it.
