# Soulcalibur VI — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Bandai Namco (producer Motohiro Okubo)
- Genre / platforms: 3D weapons-based fighting game / PS4, Xbox One, PC (Steam)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Okubo's guiding question was "What's fun about [using swords]?" — the feel of swinging a sword, not fists. VI was built as a loving reunion: merging SCII's "light movement and feel-good aspect of waving the sword around" with SCV's high-level battle system, and "cannot betray the previous fans" with radical change. The character creator is "a very essential part of the Soul Calibur experience" — players make *their* fighter. Guest characters (Geralt first) chosen for character and fun, with "balance is last." And Okubo's retention promise is explicit and humble: "What we need to do for the community is to keep providing new content and news so the community can expand more... we want to add more new stages, new game modes, and improvements." After release, "the game itself is up to each user" — the players' use of the battle system "has overcome our expectations."
- Key quotes:
  - "First of all, I thought with Soul Calibur VI that we cannot betray the previous fans of Soul Calibur with radical change in the texture and feeling of playing... what we want to achieve in Soul Calibur VI is to merge the light movement and feel-good aspect of waving the sword around the air from Soul Calibur II with the high-level battle system from Soul Calibur V." — Motohiro Okubo, Producer, Gamereactor interview (2018)
  - "I think knowing the character that will be the guest, knowing and respecting the original is important. We want to add and have fun with each guest character and balance is last. The character has to be fun and needs distinct characteristics for us to use." — Motohiro Okubo, Producer, Dot Esports interview (~2019)
  - "After we released the game, the game itself is up to each user... how [players] use the battle system has overcome our expectations. So all I have is a thank you for the community." — Motohiro Okubo, Producer, Dot Esports interview (~2019)
  - "What we need to do for the community is to keep providing new content and news so the community can expand more." — Motohiro Okubo, Producer, Dot Esports interview (~2019)
- Why players love/support it: The beloved weapons-based 3D combat identity; deep character creation (their own fighters, with guests' styles like Geralt's usable); the Libra of Soul story RPG mode; guest characters (Geralt, 2B, Haohmaru); the community's loyalty to a 20+ year series that finally got a "what is SOULCALIBUR" reunion entry.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Premium + season pass (characters + "over a hundred" creation parts). Notably fair on events: Okubo said he "wouldn't want to" gate special costumes behind limited activities that exclude players who missed them — "He would rather launch these special costumes in the form of DLCs" (GamerBraves, 2018) — i.e., everyone gets the same buy-in, no FOMO lockouts.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Weapon-based combat has a distinct *feel* — protect the tactile identity that makes players pick your game over a fists-only brawler. And respect the player's time: make cosmetics buyable for everyone, never gated behind limited windows that punish players who weren't there.
