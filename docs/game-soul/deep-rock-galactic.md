# Deep Rock Galactic — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Ghost Ship Games (published by Coffee Stain Studios; now self-publishing via Ghost Ship Publishing)
- Genre / platforms: 4-player co-op FPS; PC, Xbox, PlayStation (2018)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): The co-op working-together design — a "working environment where you're all working in the mines together" — sets a positive tone that through pure game design manufactured the friendliest community in gaming; "open development" (transparency + player involvement in future updates) built a two-way dialogue that deepened loyalty; fair, generous live service — seasons that return so new players can play past content, so every hour played turns a player into an evangelist who brings friends.
- Key quotes:
  - "We concluded that it's actually possible, through game design, to create a positive community, which is super nice to know." — Søren Lundgaard, CEO, Ghost Ship Games, NME (2024)
  - "I don't feel like [the community is] a resource, I feel like it's a dialogue." [unverified] — Alex Skronski, Head of Publishing and Marketing, Ghost Ship Publishing, Gamereactor (2026; search snippet only)
  - "We call it elbow grease marketing... Spending time and energy on showing and telling what it is we're doing to the community and listening to the community and having that dialogue." [unverified] — Alex Skronski, Head of Publishing and Marketing, Ghost Ship Publishing, Gamereactor (2026; search snippet only)
  - "By making it possible to play past seasons, we are expecting new players to keep playing Deep Rock Galactic for even more hours than they did before. And every hour played equals a fan who is continuously engaged with the game. They will get their friends to play and they will evangelize the game." [unverified] — Søren Lundgaard, CEO, Ghost Ship Games, Screen Rant (2023; search snippet only)
- Why players love/support it: "Rock and Stone!" — the community rallying cry is a wholesome internet meme; 8M+ copies, 500k+ Discord, famously zero-toxicity playerbase; generous devs who never sunset seasonal content and treat players as partners; co-op-only design means strangers help each other instead of griefing.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No direct developer quote on monetization philosophy located in this pass. ANALYSIS: buy-to-play with free seasons; cosmetic DLC only; players routinely praise the game as non-predatory ("the devs aren't trying to shake you down") — a stance that itself became a selling point.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Positivity is a design decision, not luck — co-op "work together" framing manufactures a wholesome community the way PvP manufactures toxicity. Never sunset content: returning old seasons turns every new player into a lifelong fan who evangelizes to friends — generosity compounds.
