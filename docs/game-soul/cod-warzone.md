# Call of Duty: Warzone — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Infinity Ward / Raven Software (Activision)
- Genre / platforms: Battle royale FPS; PC, PS4/PS5, Xbox (free-to-play, launched 2020)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Devs deliberately broke the "big map, one life" rule — second-chance mechanics (Gulag, buybacks) keep squads engaged all the way to the final circle instead of quitting after death; rotating limited-time modes and respawn experiments keep the loop fresh; a "core BR + limited-time mode" rotation cadence governed by what the data says players actually enjoy.
- Key quotes:
  - "We don't see *Warzone* as a battle royale. We see it as just big-map game modes, right? So we're constantly pushing things beyond battle royale, like you're seeing in Plunder." — Amos Hodge, Creative Director, Raven Software, Engadget, 2020
  - "In the original battle royale games, when you were dead, you were out... We really liked the idea of putting that power in the player's hands. And it was also a nice, fair, second chance." — Joe Cecot, Multiplayer Design Director, Infinity Ward, Engadget, 2020
  - "That definitely made it more frenetic, lots of people coming back, lots of revenge fights, which are fun. I think as we continue, you'll see us playing more and more with respawn mechanics." — Amos Hodge, Creative Director, Raven Software, Engadget, 2020
  - "We look at popularity, and the Stimulus one was doing really well so we left it on through the weekend and further... Eventually we do want to get to the point where we have kind of like our core BR and then we have our limited-time mode that we're running." — Joe Cecot, Multiplayer Design Director, Infinity Ward, Engadget, 2020
- Why players love/support it: Free-to-play with one of the smoothest gunfeels in shooters; the Gulag is a uniquely *Call of Duty* twist nobody else has; constant live updates and rotating modes; cross-platform squads keep friend groups together; consistent narrative continuity across CoD titles.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No on-record developer statement on monetization philosophy found for Warzone in this pass. ANALYSIS: free-to-play funded by battle pass and cosmetic store; no pay-to-win on record from dev statements.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Kill the "you're out, watch your friends have fun" dead time — build second-chance mechanics into every competitive loop (buyback, mini-duel, revive race) so a lost fight is a beat, not an exit. Rotate one limited-time variant alongside the core loop, and let player data — not gut — decide what stays.
