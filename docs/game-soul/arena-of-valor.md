# Arena of Valor — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: TiMi Studio (Tencent); global title, Honor of Kings in China; launched China 2015, global 2016-17; 200M+ registered players; cultural phenomenon in Thailand and Vietnam
- Genre / platforms: Mobile MOBA (5v5) / iOS, Android
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Three pillars — (1) a team with deep genre understanding that innovates rapidly (new content, competitive integrity, feel optimizations); (2) a very high bar for quality in art, hero design, gameplay, with "globalisation of MOBA as a mobile game" as the mission; (3) global competitive integrity plus regional content (local managers pick market-specific content — Colonel Sanders Ormarr skin in Taiwan, Thai-boxing Raz skin). Experience over data: new markets are judged on whether the localized game "really brings to local players a good gaming experience," not just metrics.
- Key quotes:
  - "First is our team at TiMi Studios has a deep understanding of the genre. We can innovate and make changes rapidly to help us deliver more fun for players. Whether that's delivering new content, maintaining the competitive integrity of the game or optimising the overall experience to make it feel better, our team is incredibly talented and motivated to deliver a great game for players." — Ray Ning, executive producer, PocketGamer.biz, 2020
  - "The Arena of Valor team has always set a high bar for quality, even pointing the way for the globalisation of MOBA as a mobile game as its mission." — Ray Ning, executive producer, PocketGamer.biz, 2020
  - "When we launch in new territories, we do not pursue success only in data but care more if the localised game can really bring to local players a good gaming experience." — Ray Ning, executive producer, PocketGamer.biz, 2020
  - "The perception we are trying very hard to encourage is that true competition can happen on the mobile platform, and Arena of Valor is proof of that." — Tencent, ComicBook.com interview, 2018
- Why players love/support it: Proof that real competition happens on mobile; tight controls and skill-based fairness (no pay-to-win heroes — outcomes are the result of player skill, not imbalanced parameters); fast-paced matches; regional identity content (DC heroes in the West, Thai-boxing skins); esports ecosystem across SEA and Europe.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): No direct dev quote on monetization philosophy found. Hero roster is the monetization vehicle — but competitive fairness is protected: "Players will keep playing the game only if they are sure that everything that happens in the arena is the result of player's skills and not a consequence of imbalanced hero parameters" (Vadim Bulatov design teardown, Medium, 2018 — ANALYSIS). No on-record TiMi statement on F2P philosophy.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Set a punishingly high quality bar for the core experience, then split global competitive integrity from local flavor — one shared game, many regional souls. Players stay when they trust outcomes are earned, not bought.
