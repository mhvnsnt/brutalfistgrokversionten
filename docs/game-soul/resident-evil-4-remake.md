# Resident Evil 4 (2023 remake) — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Capcom (Divisions led by directors Yasuhiro Ampo and Kazunori Kadoi; producer Yoshiaki Hirabayashi)
- Genre / platforms: Survival-horror / third-person action; PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, PC (2023)
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): Player choice and replayability. Directors Ampo and Kadoi identify the original RE4's varied combat options as "among their favorite aspects" and the primary reason for its sustained popularity — "within the confines of fighting, the combat is really well done, and the choices you have when fighting are rather varied." For the remake they deliberately expanded choice *outside* combat (stealth, bolt thrower, new paths, side quests) "to ensure the remake would be entertaining after multiple playthroughs." Hirabayashi's philosophy: preserve what was good in the original, arrange it for modern games, and think constantly about "what would fans love? What do we need to preserve? What can we power up?" The ultimate dev test: the devs themselves couldn't put it down — "no matter how much I played it, I never became bored. That's an important core factor."
- Key quotes: (2-4; each: "quote" — Name, Role, Publication/Event, Year; append [unverified] if from a search snippet not a fetched page)
  - "Within the confines of fighting, the combat is really well done, and the choices you have when fighting are rather varied... But outside of that, we felt there wasn't a lot of choice and thought there was room for updates there." — Yasuhiro Ampo, director, Game Informer, 2023
  - "Making a game that you have fun playing yourself is important. If you're making a game, you play it over and over during the course of development, and eventually, you'll probably start to get bored. But with the remake of RE2, no matter how much I played it, I never became bored. That's an important core factor." — Yasuhiro Ampo, director, Game Informer, 2023
  - "One hint we got from the RE2 remake was how to preserve the game in a way that makes fans the happiest... we went in and looked at the game and asked, 'What would fans love? What do we need to preserve? What can we have the same? What can we power up?'" — Yoshiaki Hirabayashi, producer, ComingSoon.net (via GameDaily-style preview), 2022 [unverified]
  - "The comprehensive and fundamental understanding of what it was that made the original work in the first place is probably the most important point of a good remake." — Shinji Mikami, original RE4 director, IGN interview (via Game8 summary), 2024 [unverified]
- Why players love/support it: Variety of gameplay content, legendary status as the defining third-person action template, Mercenaries mode, modding, the combat-choice sandbox, and Leon/Merchant charm. It sells millions on each re-release because the formula is evergreen.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Not stated directly by devs on record; Capcom strategy is premium full-price releases plus DLC (Separate Ways), Gold Editions, and long-tail platform ports — no live-service mechanics.
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Build combat that gives players *many valid approaches* (shoot, stealth, parry, knife) — that's the replayability engine. And the dev-shipping test is real: if the team gets bored of their own game during development, players will too.
