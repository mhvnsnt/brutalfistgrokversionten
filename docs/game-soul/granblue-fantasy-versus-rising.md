# Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising — Heart and Soul
- Developer / studio: Arc System Works (dev); Cygames (producer/IP) — creative director Tetsuya Fukuhara; executive director Yuito Kimura
- Genre / platforms: Anime fighting game (with Grand Bruise party mode) / PS4, PS5, PC (Steam); Free Edition available
- Why players come back (in the devs' view): "Fighting games are more fun the more people are available to play" — the entire design and business model flows from that one line. Fukuhara pushed the Free Edition (4 rotating free characters, online lobbies, Grand Bruise, story samples) because he believes lobbies must stay lively: "you can potentially fight forever without even needing as many as 4 characters." Accessibility is the design philosophy: simple inputs with equal properties to technical inputs ("the joy of fighting games... to as many people as possible"), while depth lives in strategy and cooldown management rather than execution. Grand Bruise! (a Fall-Guys-like party mode) exists to give casual players a reason to hang out in the same game as the fighters: "we hope that the players of the two games will mingle, leading Grand Bruise players to take an interest in fighting games." Cygames's Kimura ties retention to their mobile-game expertise: "We did a lot of things and were successful in keeping our players devoted to the game for a long time, so we just want to replicate that into the fighting game as well."
- Key quotes:
  - "Fighting games are more fun the more people are available to play. We actually wanted to do this for the previous game. So we've made big parts of the fighting experience free, as well as things that give you a taste of Granblue Fantasy like online lobbies, parts of story mode, and more." — Tetsuya Fukuhara, Creative Director, Famitsu interview (via Granblue_EN, Dec 2023)
  - "Fighting games are really all about your experiences with other players, and you can potentially fight forever without even needing as many as 4 characters. Grand Bruise! is another mode that is better with lots of players at a time, so we want our lobbies to stay lively with lots of players and don't mind making that part free." — Tetsuya Fukuhara, Creative Director, Famitsu interview (via Granblue_EN, Dec 2023)
  - "Looking beyond skill inputs, we want to bring the fun of fighting games—like reading your opponent or navigating moment-to-moment strategy—to players far and wide. We expect this will only bolster the fighting game community, so we're going to do everything in our power to bring you the best version of GBVSR that we possibly can." — Tetsuya Fukuhara, Creative Director, official message on input changes (via FinalWeapon, Jul 2023)
  - "As you mentioned, Granblue Fantasy is 5 years old now. We did a lot of things and were successful in keeping our players devoted to the game for a long time, so we just want to replicate that into the fighting game as well." — Yuito Kimura, Executive Director, RPG Site interview, Anime Expo 2019
- Why players love/support it: The 50/50 audience (fighting-game fans + Granblue mobile/anime fans); gorgeous anime visuals; the party mode that keeps the game social; rollback netcode; story mode that respects the IP; generous Free Edition lowers the "try it" bar to zero.
- Monetization philosophy (devs' words, if any): Free Edition as a funnel — "that's not something you often see within the fighting game genre, and so Cygames as a company wanted to challenge this" (Fukuhara, Gematsu, 2023). Players convert to full purchases + DLC characters/weapon skins; carrying over trophies/colors/skins rewards prior investment. Fukuhara: "Releasing a free edition... Cygames as a company wanted to challenge this and see where it goes."
- Lesson for our games (1-2 lines, concrete): Make the social/shared parts of the game free to keep lobbies lively — "fighting games are more fun the more people are available to play." And give casual players a reason to stay in the same game as the competitive ones (party/casual modes funnel into the core).
