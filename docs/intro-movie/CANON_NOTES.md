# Canon notes: owner-stated facts (intro FMV, 2026-09-28)

These facts come directly from the owner (GitHub `mhvnsnt`) during intro-FMV production on 2026-09-28 (CT). They're owner canon, and they take precedence over older repo text where the two differ. Nothing here is invented. Where repo files add context, I cite them.

## Factions
- **Onyx leads a third, separate dark "chaos" faction: Onyx, Static, Hollow, Cipher, and Echo.** It belongs to neither AWE nor Corporate/JPCW, and it sits **outside the normal book and canon order**.
  - Context: the Bannon repo also treats this group as game-only and separate from the six books (`canon/godwithin/noncanon_roster.md` in `mhvnsnt/Bannon`). The blueprint's older line, "Chaos faction: Static, Kobra, Hollow", is superseded by the owner's list above.
- **Finxsse is a flip-flopper.** He stands **opposite Bannon** in the face-off (Great Banyan Tree, C1–C2).

## Theory
- **Theory** is a **future female duo partner for Onyx.** She treats Onyx like a leashed guard dog, with dominatrix vibes, but **Onyx is secretly and ominously the real boss.** Onyx never shows this until Theory appears.
- Theory **has no in-game model yet.** The owner has made **two Tripo attires**, but neither has been imported into the game repo.
- **Theory is excluded from the intro FMV.** Onyx shows no hint of the Theory dynamic in the FMV.

## Stick-Up
- **Cyborg Stick-Up** is a **Terminator-style model that the owner will build.** It's MODEL_MISSING until it lands.
- In the intro FMV he wears Cyborg attire **only in the Banyan Tree scenes (C1–C3).** He uses his normal `STICKUP.glb` look everywhere else. Until the owner's model exists, the Cyborg look is a flagged stand-in: the default model plus a post/VFX half-face metal overlay and a red eye.
  - Context: Cyborg Stick-Up is book canon in the Bannon repo (Book 1 coda, and Book 2's "Cyborg File" vignettes with "visible cybernetic modifications").
